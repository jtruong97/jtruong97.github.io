Track a series

Search any series and see whether it is actually complete before you start it.

Why I built it

I kept finishing the last published book in a series, hitting a cliffhanger, and only then realizing the series was unfinished — sometimes abandoned years earlier. I tracked what I was reading in a notes app, which made it easy to lose a series entirely, and researching whether something was complete before starting took real effort and was often wrong.

So the app answers it directly. It will tell you that A Song of Ice and Fire is still ongoing, that the last book landed in 2012, and that nothing has been announced in the fourteen years since.

Under the hood

Scheduled Supabase Edge Functions keep ~15,000 titles current — discovering new releases from followed authors and backfilling metadata in bounded batches, with oldest-first queue rotation and a rate-limit circuit breaker so runs resume where they stopped. Metadata is reconciled across three external APIs with per-field fallback, validating publication dates against an independently-sourced anchor year rather than last-write-wins. Search hits the Postgres catalog through a fuzzy-match RPC before falling back to those APIs, ranked so a series name surfaces its sequels. Deduplication runs in PL/pgSQL via pg_cron, where dry runs against production data caught three matching rules with 85–99% false positives before any destructive merge. Auth is OAuth 2.0 and JWT with row-level security, making shelves and ratings private by default.

On your phone

Installs to the home screen and runs full-screen — no app store needed. On iPhone, open fully-bookd.com in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open the browser menu and choose Install app.
