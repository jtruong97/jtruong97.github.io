I'm Jasmine Truong, a Full-Stack Software Engineer

I'm a full-stack software engineer building React and TypeScript applications for an AI platform used by national security customers. What I enjoy most is taking a rough idea and turning it into something people actually use — working through the ambiguity with data engineers, figuring out the design, and shipping it. I build features end to end now, and I was promoted to Software Engineer II in under two years.

I started out in healthcare as a medical assistant with a B.S. in biology. I switched to software because I love solving problems and building things from scratch, and I haven't looked back. Everything I know about engineering I learned by being a quick study and shipping — starting at App Academy and continuing every day since.

On the side I built FullyBookd, a reading tracker for upcoming book releases that I use every day. It came out of a problem I kept hitting as a reader, and it's the clearest example of how I like to work: notice something missing, design it, build it, ship it.

Outside of work I read constantly and I'm closing in on a goal of 100 books, so recommendations are always welcome. I'm deep into sourdough baking and bead embroidery, I golf every so often, and I'm obsessed with matcha — I'll try a new matcha café anywhere. I travel internationally once or twice a year and I'm a lifelong foodie, so I'm always up for restaurant recommendations around Northern California, in either direction.

I'm currently open to new opportunities. I grew up in San Francisco and now split my time between the Bay Area and Sacramento — always happy to connect with engineers in either.
